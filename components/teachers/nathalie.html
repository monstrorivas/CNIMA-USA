Nathalie Boucheix

Nathalie Boucheix knows CNIMA from both sides of the music stand. She grew up in Auvergne and studied accordion with Jacques Mornet as a teenager. In 1995 the two of them co-founded CNIMA, and today she's its director.

Before she was running a school, though, she was winning just about everything in sight. In 1993 she took the Médard Ferrero Prize and the SACEM Francis Baxter Prize. The next year she won two of the most prestigious accordion competitions in the world, the Trophée Mondial and Klingenthal. With her advanced diploma (DEM) in accordion in hand, she made a name for herself early on as a brilliant, exacting performer.

She has played many of France's big gala concerts, sharing the stage with legends of French accordion like André Verchuren, Maurice Larcange and Yvette Horner. She also gives recitals around the world, playing a concert-variety repertoire with a refined, contemporary sound.

At CNIMA, Nathalie wears many hats. She's the administrative director, the pedagogical director, and a teacher of both accordion and musicianship. She's known as an expert in technique and interpretation, and she trains musicians from every kind of background, in both classical and popular repertoire. She and Jacques also lead workshops and masterclasses together, in France and around the world.

The results speak for themselves as CNIMA's students continue to reflect the school's brilliant musical approach with their dominance in the accordion world.

In 2025, at the Coupe Mondiale, the Confédération Internationale des Accordéonistes (CIA) gave Nathalie its Merit Award for her exceptional contribution to the accordion's international reach. It puts her in fine company, with earlier recipients including the Russian master Friedrich Lips and the world-famous virtuoso Richard Galliano.

We're lucky to have her with us in New Orleans.
