About CNIMA

For the fifth year, CNIMA will be hosting workshops in New Orleans, Louisiana. The CNIMA (Centre National et International de Musique et d’Accordéon) is a world-renowned accordion school based in France, founded by esteemed teacher Jacques Mornet and award-winning accordionist Nathalie Boucheix.

Over the years, CNIMA students have won more than 130 first prizes in international competitions. The school has shaped several generations of extraordinary accordionists from across the globe, laying solid foundations for technical mastery and expressive playing.

At the heart of Mornet’s approach is the idea of treating the accordion as a wind instrument, not just a keyboard. By learning to shape the airflow with intention and pair it with a sensitive touch on the keys, players discover freedom in their musicality.
